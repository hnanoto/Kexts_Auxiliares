Clover kext installation guide

1. Mount your EFI partition.
2. Copy:
   - AirPortUtility.kext
   - BluetoothFileExchange.kext
   to EFI/CLOVER/kexts/Other/
3. Reboot and verify Clover injected both kexts.

Notes:
- Clover does not require OpenCore Kernel->Add entries.
- Keep both kext names exactly as distributed.
